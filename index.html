How it works

A clear process helps customers feel confident, especially for custom work.

1
Tell us what you want

Message us on WhatsApp or email with what you have in mind. If you want something custom, share a photo or a short description of the colours and style you like.

2
We confirm options and pricing

Prices are available on request. Once we understand the design and materials, we will confirm pricing, timelines, and delivery within South Africa via courier.

Contact

WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us. Email is also available for detailed enquiries.

Enquiries and orders

If you are asking about a custom piece, please include the type of item, preferred colours, and any materials you would like included, for example shells, sand, dried flowers, or small keepsakes.
